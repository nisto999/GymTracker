Niten

A quiet gym tracker.

Log your sets, watch your strength climb — with no account, no ads, and nothing uploaded anywhere.

Open Niten

Free. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

Keep it on your home screen

  1. Tap Open Niten above.
  2. On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    On Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app.
  3. It opens like an app, and works with no signal.

What it does

Logs weight and reps for eighty-plus exercises, charts your estimated one-rep max over weeks or months, maps which muscles you have been working, and times your intervals and holds.

Everything stays on your device. Back up to a file whenever you like.
